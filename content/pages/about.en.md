# About Storink

Storink publishes comic stories designed for the screen: read them panel by panel on a phone, or page by page on a larger screen.

## How the comics are made

Storink comics are created with the help of artificial intelligence. The author writes the story, the breakdown and the dialogue, checks the facts, then has the images generated page by page and corrects them until they are right. The images are not hand-drawn.

## Free, no tracking

Reading is free. Storink sets no cookies and stores no IP addresses: audience statistics are anonymous and aggregated (number of reads, pages reached, where visits come from).

## The series

- **Monsieur X**: a faceless man tells the great spy stories of the 20th century. A tribute to the radio show “Rendez-vous avec X” by Patrick Pesnot, with no connection to France Inter or Radio France. In French.
- **Napkin Ideas**: business ideas sketched over lunch (coming soon).
